# Presentation Event (Business Presentation) pre-event submission, Table PS-1 row "Presentation (if required)", S.2.4

Generated by `acceptance/submissions/make_reports.py` from the saved model. Do not edit; change the model and regenerate. The model is a candidate architecture: a link shows where a value should come from, not that the submission is complete or compliant.

The Presentation Event is a 75-point static event (S.1:p001). The team pitches a business, logistics, production or technical case for its car to a judging panel that is not all engineers (S.2.1, S.2.2.5). The rulebook does not set what the submission contains. Under S.2.4.1 and Table PS-1 row p007, a pre-event submission exists only if that year's Presentation Concept asks for one. The Concept is published on the Event Website (fsaeonline.com, Series Resources) and may change for each competition (S.2.2.1, S.2.2.2). It sets the content, file format, due date and any extra penalties (S.2.4.1, S.2.4.3). Any submission is uploaded to the team account before the Due Date by a Team Member or Team Captain. Late or missing uploads are penalised under the PS rules, in Table PS-2 group "Other". The penalty is -20 points for each 24 hours late, with any part of a day counting as a full day, applied with official discretion (PS.3.2.1, PS.1.2.6). A Not Submitted document removes the team from the event with a score of zero (PS.3.2.2, TABLE-PS-2:p004). The Concept may set different penalties (S.2.4.3). Judges may include the submission in the event score (S.2.4.2). At the event, the team presents live in its assigned slot, and the judges then ask questions (S.2.3, S.2.5). The 2027 Concept was not available for this research, so every content item is marked as defined by the Concept and still to be determined. Background material found on the web (older event guides and team pages) is not authoritative and has not been used as fact. Model state: the S.1, S.2, PS and Table PS-1/PS-2 rule paragraphs are in the model but not traced to any element. It also holds two operational activities owned by Team personnel, "FSAE2027:process Prepare and document competition entry" and "FSAE2027:process Inspect and present the vehicle". Each is only a name, with no description, exchanges, capability involvement or requirement links. The only content the model can supply is a description of the vehicle as entered (S.2.2.4), drawn from SA, LA, PA and EPBS. Its specifications are TBD because the DEC-* decisions are still open. No item is fully present in the model. The six vehicle-content items are partial: their anchor elements exist by name but carry no description or values, and the values wait on the DEC-* decisions. Every other item is absent. The model treats competition administration as a team process, so the two process activities are the anchors.

How to read the tables:

- **Model**: `present` means the candidate model already holds the content (an architecture element, state logic or a diagram) with nothing open; it does not mean the submitted value is final. `partial` means a model element or obligation stands for the item but holds no team value yet; items that only restate a rule value are `partial` too. `absent` means the model has nothing for the item, or cannot hold it (photos, raw test data, receipts, records).
- **Source**: where the value comes from: `model` (this Eclipse Capella® model), `cad` (CAD and drawings), `analysis` (calculations), `test` (physical tests), `supplier` (datasheets and purchase records), `admin` (team administration).
- **Status**: the team's progress. Conditional rows (aluminum, composite, monocoque, custom IA, digital APPS/BSE) count until you mark them `not_applicable` through the status CSV, once DEC-CHASSIS or the IA route is chosen ([how to update status](../../acceptance/submissions/README.md#updating-status)).
- **Blocking**: ⛔ marks a decision whose constraint name still ends in UNRESOLVED. [Open design decisions](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#open-design-decisions) lists them, and step 2 of [Using the model as a development platform](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#using-the-model-as-a-development-platform) says how to record and close one.
- **Note** says why an item is partial or absent, which rows are conditional and what is still missing. **Evidence** is the drawing, file, photo or test id the team records.
