# General submission rules (PS.1-PS.3 and cross-cutting admin rules): location, format, deadlines, penalties

Generated by `acceptance/submissions/make_reports.py` from the saved model. Do not edit; change the model and regenerate. The model is a candidate architecture: a link shows where a value should come from, not that the submission is complete or compliant.

This is the cross-cutting layer that applies to every pre-competition document, not a single deliverable. Table PS-1 lists the documents: SES, ESO/ESA form, ESF, Design Briefing, Vehicle Drawings, Design Spec Sheet, Cost Report, Cost Addendum, the Presentation and Cost Scenario pre-event items (only if the Presentation Concept or Cost Scenario asks for them), and the IC-only ETC forms. Only a Team Captain or an accepted Team Member may upload, into the team's own account on the Event Website (fsaeonline.com). Most documents go up as one PDF or XLSX file, and the ones Table PS-1 marks with note (1) must use the unmodified official template. There are exceptions. The Cost Report is generated inside the Event Website. The Cost Addendum is handed in at onsite registration. The Presentation and Cost Scenario items take their format from the Concept or Scenario document. Each document has its own Due Date and Cancellation Date. These are set on the live Event Website deadlines page (https://www.fsaeonline.com/Deadlines.aspx?seriesCode=FSAE) or announced by the organizer, not in the rulebook. An event official assigned as Reviewer (PS.1.2.8) reviews and accepts each submission. A late document in the Tech or Other group costs 20 points for each 24-hour period or part of one. If a Tech-group document (SES, ESO/ESA form, ESF) is never submitted, that is grounds for removing the team entry. If an Other-group document is never submitted, the team is dropped from the Cost, Design or Presentation event and scores zero there. Organizers do not warn a team about a wrong upload, so the team has to keep its own proof of upload. Model state: the 72 PS, Table PS-1 and Table PS-2 paragraphs are in the model but not traced to any element. 21 of them (every Table PS-1 and PS-2 row and the PS.3.4.1 and PS.3.4.2 headings) still need an applicability review. The only related element is the operational activity 'FSAE2027:process Prepare and document competition entry', owned by Team personnel, with no exchanges or links. The penalty numbers sit in the Table PS-2 paragraphs as unreviewed source values. The 2027 SES, ESO/ESA and Cost Addendum templates and the 2027 ESF and EV inspection forms are not in this repository yet; download them from the Event Website.

**Readiness:** 0 % (0 of 50), counting submitted and accepted items and leaving out not_applicable ones.

**Items:** 52. Model status: present 0, partial 2, absent 50. Team status: not_started 50, not_applicable 2. Blocked by an unresolved decision: 1.

How to read the tables:

- **Model**: `present` means the candidate model already holds the content (an architecture element, state logic or a diagram) with nothing open; it does not mean the submitted value is final. `partial` means a model element or obligation stands for the item but holds no team value yet; items that only restate a rule value are `partial` too. `absent` means the model has nothing for the item, or cannot hold it (photos, raw test data, receipts, records).
- **Source**: where the value comes from: `model` (this Eclipse Capella® model), `cad` (CAD and drawings), `analysis` (calculations), `test` (physical tests), `supplier` (datasheets and purchase records), `admin` (team administration).
- **Status**: the team's progress. Conditional rows (aluminum, composite, monocoque, custom IA, digital APPS/BSE) count until you mark them `not_applicable` through the status CSV, once DEC-CHASSIS or the IA route is chosen ([how to update status](../../acceptance/submissions/README.md#updating-status)).
- **Blocking**: ⛔ marks a decision whose constraint name still ends in UNRESOLVED. [Open design decisions](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#open-design-decisions) lists them, and step 2 of [Using the model as a development platform](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#using-the-model-as-a-development-platform) says how to record and close one.
- **Note** says why an item is partial or absent, which rows are conditional and what is still missing. **Evidence** is the drawing, file, photo or test id the team records.

## Governing rules

- PS.1.1.1-PS.1.1.2: every document is due by the deadlines and procedures the Event Website or the organizer publishes
- PS.1.2.1-PS.1.2.8: meaning of submission date, due date, cancellation date, late, not submitted (wrong format or largely incomplete counts), days late rounded up, removal grounds and Reviewer
- PS.2.1, PS.2.2, PS.2.2.1-PS.2.2.4: upload through the team's Event Website account; Table PS-1 templates used unchanged; one PDF or XLSX file per submission
- PS.3.1.1-PS.3.1.4: the team confirms its own uploads; free replacement before the due date; a revision request can set a new deadline; no warning for a wrong upload
- PS.3.2.1-PS.3.2.3: late penalties per Table PS-2, plus further penalties for missing or poorly reviewed documents at official discretion
- PS.3.3.1-PS.3.3.2: an entry can be removed for a missing grounds-for-removal document, no reply to the Reviewer or organizer, or at organizer discretion; notice is given and fees are not refunded
- PS.3.4.1-PS.3.4.2, IC.4.2: IC-only cases (ETC forms, fuel type); not applicable to this electric car
- TABLE-PS-1:p001, TABLE-PS-2:p001: the list of documents with format, template and penalty group, and the penalty per group
- AD.1.1-AD.1.3: events may post local rule variations; teams follow official announcements; English is accepted
- AD.2.1, AD.2.1.1-AD.2.1.4, AD.2.2: the Event Website and its Series Resources, the team account, the Team Captain, who may upload, and how to reach the organizer
- AD.3.7, AD.4.1: student career profiles and the registration roster deadline
- AD.5.1.3: the Faculty Advisor must not write team documents or presentations
- AD.5.2.2, AD.5.3.2: ESO training and ESA experience go on the ESO/ESA form
- AD.6.1, AD.6.2.1-AD.6.2.2, AD.6.3.1-AD.6.3.2, AD.6.4: registration details, fees, waitlist deadlines and withdrawal
- GR.4.2.3, GR.4.5.1: documents incorporated by reference count as rules; follow the latest rules issue
- GR.7.1, GR.7.2.1-GR.7.2.3, GR.7.3: rules questions: scope, required content, response time, where to send them, and that answers may be published
- IN.5.1, IN.6.1: the ESF with datasheets and the electronic SES are brought to inspection
- EV.2.1.1, EV.2.1.3, EV.2.2, F.2.2.1-F.2.2.2: ESF and SES are required on the given format with PS penalties; SES reviewer comments must be closed
- S.2.4.1, S.2.4.3, S.3.4.2, S.3.6, S.3.7.2-S.3.7.3, S.3.15.3, S.3.15.5: presentation, cost report, cost addendum and cost scenario submissions and their PS penalties
- S.4.2.3-S.4.2.4, S.4.3.1, S.4.3.3, S.4.4.2, S.4.5: design documents follow PS, use the templates and must show a serious effort
