# Cost Report (Cost and Manufacturing Event, with its Cost Addendum and Cost Scenario companions)

Generated by `acceptance/submissions/make_reports.py` from the saved model. Do not edit; change the model and regenerate. The model is a candidate architecture: a link shows where a value should come from, not that the submission is complete or compliant.

The Cost Report is the team's priced bill of materials for the whole prototype. It is built and uploaded in the Event Website's cost tool, in whatever form that site specifies (Table PS-1 note 2), and it is due before the competition at the Due Date posted on the Event Website. The Cost Event is worth 100 points in total (S.3.16.2), and the Cost Judges assess the report against the car at the competition (S.3.13.4). At the competition the judges hold a discussion beside the car, comparing the report with the vehicle as presented and judging whether the car could be manufactured. Teams may also be picked for a Cost Audit. Table PS-1 puts the Cost Report in penalty group Other. Under Table PS-2 that means -20 points for each 24 hours late, with any part of a day counted as a full day. A report not submitted by the Cancellation Date removes the team from the Cost event with a score of zero. Every price must come from the official Cost Tables, built up by vehicle, then system, then assembly, then part, then material, process, fastener and tooling. The Cost Event Supplement on the Event Website carries the detailed costing guidance (S.3.2); the public Supplement is where the system categories and tooling method are defined. Two related deliverables follow. The Cost Addendum is handed in at onsite registration on a template and covers changes made after submission. The Cost Scenario submission is required only if that year's scenario document asks for it, and Table PS-1 also puts it in penalty group Other. Penalties work two ways. Method A deducts a fixed number of points per omission. Method B adds twice the understated amount to the Adjusted Cost. Whichever is larger applies. The model already has the vehicle-to-subsystem-to-CI tree (EPBS) and a physical-node tree (PA). It holds no cost, material, process, fastener, tooling, make/buy or quantity data. None of the 90 S.1/S.3 rule paragraphs, the 53 PS paragraphs or the 19 Table PS-1/PS-2 rows is traced to a model element yet.

How to read the tables:

- **Model**: `present` means the candidate model already holds the content (an architecture element, state logic or a diagram) with nothing open; it does not mean the submitted value is final. `partial` means a model element or obligation stands for the item but holds no team value yet; items that only restate a rule value are `partial` too. `absent` means the model has nothing for the item, or cannot hold it (photos, raw test data, receipts, records).
- **Source**: where the value comes from: `model` (this Capella model), `cad` (CAD and drawings), `analysis` (calculations), `test` (physical tests), `supplier` (datasheets and purchase records), `admin` (team administration).
- **Status**: the team's progress. Conditional rows (aluminum, composite, monocoque, custom IA, digital APPS/BSE) count until you mark them `not_applicable` through the status CSV, once DEC-CHASSIS or the IA route is chosen ([how to update status](../../acceptance/submissions/README.md#updating-status)).
- **Blocking**: ⛔ marks a decision whose constraint name still ends in UNRESOLVED. [Open design decisions](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#open-design-decisions) lists them, and step 2 of [Using the model as a development platform](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#using-the-model-as-a-development-platform) says how to record and close one.
- **Note** says why an item is partial or absent, which rows are conditional and what is still missing. **Evidence** is the drawing, file, photo or test id the team records.
