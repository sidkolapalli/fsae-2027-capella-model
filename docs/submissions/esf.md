# Electrical System Form (ESF)

Generated by `acceptance/submissions/make_reports.py` from the saved model. Do not edit; change the model and regenerate. The model is a candidate architecture: a link shows where a value should come from, not that the submission is complete or compliant.

The ESF is the EV-only technical document in which the team describes the complete vehicle electrical system (tractive system, GLV and control) in a structured way (EV.2.1.1). It is filed before the competition on the official SAE-supplied XLSX template, with the format unchanged, as a single file uploaded through the team's Event Website account by the posted due date (EV.2.1.3, PS.2, TABLE-PS-1:p006). An event official assigned as Reviewer checks and accepts it. Reviewers may ask for revisions with new deadlines (PS.1.2.8, PS.3.1.3). The ESO/ESA form (the Electrical System Officer and Advisor details, PDF, Tech group) is a separate upload with the same removal risk; it is tracked as GEN-PS1-02 on the general page. Before upload, the team's Electrical System Advisor must review it (AD.5.3.6). It is in the Tech penalty group: -20 points per 24 h late, and if it is still missing at the cancellation date, that is grounds for removing the entry (EV.2.2, TABLE-PS-2:p003, PS.3.3.1). Approval does not guarantee passing inspection (EV.2.1.1:p002). A copy plus component datasheets must be brought to accumulator and charger inspection (IN.5.1:p006), and the built pack and spares must match it (EV.5.1.3). The rulebook names only two content items as ESF-specific: BMS module-boundary isolation (EV.8.3.2) and pack configuration identity (EV.5.1.3). The rest of this list is derived from the EV and T rules the form must describe. The tab layout of the 2027 workbook is not public without a team login, so the section headings here are our own, not the template's. FSAE-2027-V1 has no general FMEA requirement. Failure-mode write-ups appear only for digital APPS/BSE transmission and are tied to the ETC Systems Form (T.4.2.11:p002, T.4.3.5:p002, IC.4.4.9). T.4.3.5:p002 is not marked IC-only, so an EV team that sends the BSE signal over a bus should confirm through a Rules Question where to document it.

How to read the tables:

- **Model**: `present` means the candidate model already holds the content (an architecture element, state logic or a diagram) with nothing open; it does not mean the submitted value is final. `partial` means a model element or obligation stands for the item but holds no team value yet; items that only restate a rule value are `partial` too. `absent` means the model has nothing for the item, or cannot hold it (photos, raw test data, receipts, records).
- **Source**: where the value comes from: `model` (this Capella model), `cad` (CAD and drawings), `analysis` (calculations), `test` (physical tests), `supplier` (datasheets and purchase records), `admin` (team administration).
- **Status**: the team's progress. Conditional rows (aluminum, composite, monocoque, custom IA, digital APPS/BSE) count until you mark them `not_applicable` through the status CSV, once DEC-CHASSIS or the IA route is chosen ([how to update status](../../acceptance/submissions/README.md#updating-status)).
- **Blocking**: ⛔ marks a decision whose constraint name still ends in UNRESOLVED. [Open design decisions](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#open-design-decisions) lists them, and step 2 of [Using the model as a development platform](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#using-the-model-as-a-development-platform) says how to record and close one.
- **Note** says why an item is partial or absent, which rows are conditional and what is still missing. **Evidence** is the drawing, file, photo or test id the team records.

## Governing rules

- EV.2.1.1 - every team submits an ESF giving clearly structured documentation of the full electrical system, TS and control included; approval does not mean the car passes inspection
- EV.2.1.2 - the ESF may carry guidance or detail beyond the rulebook
- EV.2.1.3 - use the provided format and submit per section PS
- EV.2.2 - ESF penalties follow section PS
- TABLE-PS-1:p006 / TABLE-PS-1:p014 - ESF: XLSX, Tech penalty group, use the Event Website template
- TABLE-PS-2:p003 - Tech group: -20 points per 24 h late; missing after the deadline is grounds for removal
- PS.1.1.1, PS.1.2.2-PS.1.2.8 - due date, cancellation date, late, not-submitted, removal and Reviewer definitions
- PS.2.1, PS.2.2.1-PS.2.2.4 - upload to team account; template format unaltered; one file
- PS.3.1.1-PS.3.1.4, PS.3.2.1-PS.3.2.3, PS.3.3.1-PS.3.3.2 - team responsibility, revision requests, penalties and entry removal
- AD.5.3.6 - ESA reviews the ESF and confirms sound engineering practice in principle; AD.5.3.7 - ESA makes sure unusual design aspects are communicated
- IN.5.1:p006 - ESF and component datasheets brought to accumulator/charger inspection
- EV.5.1.3 - pack components, containers and spares identical to the design in the ESF and SES
- EV.8.3.2 - BMS galvanic isolation at module boundaries is approved through the ESF
- Content rules described by the form: EV.1, EV.3-EV.10, T.3.3, T.4.2, T.4.3, T.5.3, T.9.1-T.9.4, IN.10 (expected test results); F.10 and F.11 by cross-reference to the SES
