# Impact Attenuator Data (IAD) report - section of the Structural Equivalency Spreadsheet (SES)

Generated by `acceptance/submissions/make_reports.py` from the saved model. Do not edit; change the model and regenerate. The model is a candidate architecture: a link shows where a value should come from, not that the submission is complete or compliant.

The IAD is not a standalone upload: F.8.8.1 makes it part of the SES. The SES is a single XLSX file built on the event-provided template with its format left unchanged (FSAE-2027-V1:TABLE-PS-1:p002 and p014, PS.2.2.1-PS.2.2.4). North American teams upload it through their own team login on the Event Website before the published Due Date; a later Cancellation Date is the last chance to upload (PS.1.1.1, PS.1.2.2, PS.1.2.3, PS.2.1). It is a pre-competition submission and the rulebook does not fix the date. Each submission is reviewed by an assigned event official (PS.1.2.8). Every SES reviewer comment must be resolved (F.2.2.2), and a requested revision can carry its own new deadline (PS.3.1.3). The SES sits in the Tech penalty group: -20 points per 24 h late, and a missing SES after the Cancellation Date is grounds for removing the team entry (FSAE-2027-V1:TABLE-PS-2:p003, PS.1.2.7, PS.3.3.1). A file in the wrong format or largely incomplete counts as not submitted (PS.1.2.5), and teams are not told if an upload is wrong (PS.3.1.4). Because the upload is one file, photos, test data and calculations must live inside that workbook or be referenced from it (an inference from PS.2.2.4). Content depends on the attenuator route. With an approved Standard IA no test data is sent, but photos of the real part, supplier evidence against the Standard IA specification and the remaining requirements of the section are still due (F.8.8.3; the rule says 'this section' without naming F.8 or F.8.8). A Custom IA needs test data, derivation calculations, a test schematic and photos annotated with heights before and after the test (F.8.8.4), under the validity, setup, conduct and analysis rules of F.8.8.5-F.8.8.8. The SES (not necessarily the IAD tab) must also document the AIP-to-bulkhead and IA attachment methods (F.8.2.3, F.8.5.1), and the front-object and combined-force evidence of F.8.6-F.8.7 belongs in the same front-chassis review. At Technical Inspection the team brings the electronic SES and, when applicable, the tested attenuator and any laminate test specimens (IN.6.1). The car must be built as the SES describes it (F.2.5). Model state: none of the 135 F.8 rule paragraphs is traced directly. The vehicle-protection slice restates them as FSAE2027:VP:obligation:* requirements, which have a candidate owner but no design choice and no verification. The model has no separate IA, AIP or Front Bulkhead element. The whole front stack is one lumped candidate, 'FSAE2027:VP Attenuator anti-intrusion and front load paths' (Equipment / Physical behavior / Logical subsystem / CI). No element carries property values, and the only LiteralNumericValues are predefined datatype bounds. Rule thresholds exist only as text in those obligations, so items that only restate a rule value are 'partial', not 'present'. None of the 8 DEC-* decisions covers standard-vs-custom IA or AIP material/attachment, so those choices have no DEC id; they appear only as 'Open choices' text in the VP verification constraints.

How to read the tables:

- **Model**: `present` means the candidate model already holds the content (an architecture element, state logic or a diagram) with nothing open; it does not mean the submitted value is final. `partial` means a model element or obligation stands for the item but holds no team value yet; items that only restate a rule value are `partial` too. `absent` means the model has nothing for the item, or cannot hold it (photos, raw test data, receipts, records).
- **Source**: where the value comes from: `model` (this Capella model), `cad` (CAD and drawings), `analysis` (calculations), `test` (physical tests), `supplier` (datasheets and purchase records), `admin` (team administration).
- **Status**: the team's progress. Conditional rows (aluminum, composite, monocoque, custom IA, digital APPS/BSE) count until you mark them `not_applicable` through the status CSV, once DEC-CHASSIS or the IA route is chosen ([how to update status](../../acceptance/submissions/README.md#updating-status)).
- **Blocking**: ⛔ marks a decision whose constraint name still ends in UNRESOLVED. [Open design decisions](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#open-design-decisions) lists them, and step 2 of [Using the model as a development platform](../MODEL-GUIDE.md#using-the-model-as-a-development-platform) says how to record and close one.
- **Note** says why an item is partial or absent, which rows are conditional and what is still missing. **Evidence** is the drawing, file, photo or test id the team records.

## Governing rules

- F.8.8.1: the IAD report is part of the SES, not a separate upload
- F.8.8.2: functional targets, not test criteria: deceleration within 20 g average and 40 g peak and more than 7350 J absorbed, for 300 kg at 7.0 m/s
- F.8.8.3: Standard IA: no test data; photos of the real unit and supplier proof (receipt or packing slip) against the Standard IA specification; the rest of the section still applies
- F.8.8.4: Custom IA: test data against F.8.8.2, derivation calculations, a test schematic and photos showing the height before and after the test
- F.8.8.5: a test result is valid for one competition year
- F.8.8.6: test setup: the car's IA-to-AIP joint, a representative chassis section as fixture, 50 mm free space behind the AIP, at most 25 mm permanent AIP deflection
- F.8.8.7: composite IAs need a dynamic test at a supervised dedicated facility; other IAs may also be tested quasi-statically on university equipment
- F.8.8.8: average deceleration from raw unfiltered data; CFC 60 or a 100 Hz Butterworth filter is allowed when peaks pass 40 g
- F.8.1.1-F.8.1.2: IA ahead of the Front Bulkhead with the AIP between them; both joints carry off-axis loads
- F.8.2.1-F.8.2.3: AIP of 1.5 mm steel, 4.0 mm aluminum or composite; its outline depends on the joint type; the AIP-to-bulkhead joint is documented in the SES
- F.8.3.1-F.8.3.2: a composite AIP survives a frontal impact and holds 120 kN over the minimum IA footprint, shown by a representative test or by laminate tests and calculation
- F.8.4.1-F.8.4.3: use an approved Standard IA or build and test a Custom IA with minimum size; a large bulkhead or the Standard Honeycomb needs a diagonal brace or a deflection test
- F.8.5.1-F.8.5.7: IA joint documented in the SES; allowed joint per IA type; bond, weld and bolt limits; mounting height, centering and orientation
- F.8.6.1-F.8.6.3: only the IA, fastener heads and light bodywork ahead of the AIP; front wing and tab limits; 25 mm pedal clearance to the AIP, bulkhead and non-crushable items
- F.8.7.1-F.8.7.5: IA plus the force to crush other front objects stays under the F.8.8.2 peak, using the IA peak-load list and a test or a calculation
- F.1.8, F.1.9: definitions of Front Bulkhead and Impact Attenuator
- F.2.1, F.2.2.1-F.2.2.2, F.2.3.1, F.2.5: the SES is required, reviewer comments must be closed, equivalence uses SES methods, and the car is built as the SES says
- F.3.2.1, F.6.1, F.6.4.5, F.7.2, F.7.2.3: Front Bulkhead tube size and Lower Side Impact member for a tube frame; bulkhead shear strength under the AIP for a monocoque
- F.4.2.2, F.4.2.5: laminate bend and perimeter-shear tests used by the composite AIP calculation route
- F.5.5.1-F.5.5.3: bonded joints: an adhesive suited to both surfaces, its record in the SES, and the SES halving of adhesive values
- T.8.2.1: grade of critical fasteners in bolted IA and AIP joints
- IN.6.1: bring the electronic SES, and laminate specimens if used, to Technical Inspection
- PS.1.1.1, PS.1.2.1-PS.1.2.8: posted deadlines and the meaning of due date, cancellation date, late, not submitted, days late, removal grounds and Reviewer
- PS.2.1, PS.2.2.1-PS.2.2.4, TABLE-PS-1:p002, p014: upload to the team account as one XLSX on the unchanged event template
- PS.3.1.1-PS.3.1.4, PS.3.2.1-PS.3.2.2, PS.3.3.1, TABLE-PS-2:p003: the team checks its own upload; revisions can bring new deadlines; Tech group, -20 points per day late; a missing SES is grounds for removal
